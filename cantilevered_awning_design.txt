CANTILEVERED MONOPITCH AWNING - STEEL FRAME DESIGN
=================================================

DESIGN SPECIFICATIONS:
- Material: 38mm x 38mm x 2mm SHS (Square Hollow Section)
- Length (L): 7,500 mm
- Projection (P): 800 mm
- Slope (θ): 35°
- Number of kicker braces: 5

CALCULATED DIMENSIONS:
- Rafter length: 976 mm
- Rise at front: 560 mm
- Kicker brace length: 1,131 mm (at 45° to horizontal)
- Kicker spacing: 1,500 mm centers (7,500 ÷ 5 = 1,500mm)

STRUCTURAL COMPONENTS:

1. SPINE BEAM (Main structural member)
   - Length: 7,500 mm
   - Material: 38x38x2 SHS
   - Position: Attached to wall at height to achieve 35° slope
   - Function: Primary load-bearing member, transfers loads to wall

2. WALL PLATE
   - Length: 7,500 mm  
   - Material: 38x38x2 SHS
   - Position: Fixed to wall 560mm below spine beam
   - Function: Lower attachment point, distributes loads to wall structure

3. HORIZONTAL RAFTER
   - Length: 7,500 mm
   - Material: 38x38x2 SHS
   - Position: 800mm out from wall, connecting all kicker braces
   - Function: Distributes loads between kicker braces, provides edge beam

4. KICKER BRACES (5 No.)
   - Length: 1,131 mm each
   - Material: 38x38x2 SHS
   - Angle: 45° to horizontal (approximately)
   - Spacing: 1,500mm centers
   - Position: From wall plate to horizontal rafter
   - Function: Transfer vertical loads to wall, provide structural stability

5. CONNECTING RAFTERS (Between spine and horizontal rafter)
   - Length: 976 mm each (5 No.)
   - Material: 38x38x2 SHS
   - Angle: 35° slope
   - Spacing: 1,500mm centers
   - Function: Support roofing material, transfer loads to main structure

ASSEMBLY DETAILS:

Wall Connections:
- Spine beam: Fixed to wall with appropriate brackets/bolts
- Wall plate: Fixed to wall 560mm below spine beam
- Use M12 bolts minimum, chemical anchors for masonry walls

Joint Connections:
- All tube connections via welding or mechanical connectors
- Kicker braces connect wall plate to horizontal rafter
- Connecting rafters span from spine beam to horizontal rafter
- All connections must be designed for tension and compression

LOADING CONSIDERATIONS:
- Dead load: Self weight + roofing material
- Live load: Maintenance access (0.25 kN/m²)
- Wind load: Uplift and downward pressure
- Snow load: If applicable to location

MATERIAL PROPERTIES (38x38x2 SHS):
- Section modulus: ~3.46 cm³
- Moment of inertia: ~6.58 cm⁴
- Cross-sectional area: ~2.83 cm²
- Weight: ~2.22 kg/m

CRITICAL DESIGN POINTS:
1. Wall attachment must be adequate for cantilever loads
2. Kicker braces critical for reducing bending moment in spine beam
3. All connections must resist both tension and compression
4. Consider lateral torsional buckling of spine beam
5. Deflection limits: Span/250 for permanent loads

FABRICATION NOTES:
- All cuts to be square and clean
- Weld all connections with appropriate fillet welds
- Prime and paint all steelwork for corrosion protection
- Consider pre-drilling for roofing attachment points

INSTALLATION SEQUENCE:
1. Mark and fix wall plate to wall
2. Mark and fix spine beam to wall
3. Install kicker braces between wall plate and temporary supports
4. Install horizontal rafter connecting all kicker braces
5. Install connecting rafters between spine beam and horizontal rafter
6. Check alignment and square before final welding
7. Install roofing material

PARTS LIST:
=========

38mm x 38mm x 2mm SHS:
- 1 x 7,500mm (Spine beam)
- 1 x 7,500mm (Wall plate)  
- 1 x 7,500mm (Horizontal rafter)
- 5 x 1,131mm (Kicker braces) = 5,655mm total
- 5 x 976mm (Connecting rafters) = 4,880mm total

Total SHS required: ~31,535mm = 31.5 meters

Hardware:
- Wall fixings (bolts/anchors) as per wall type
- Welding consumables or mechanical connectors
- Primer and paint system
- Roofing attachment hardware

Note: This design provides a basic structural framework. Detailed structural 
calculations should be performed by a qualified structural engineer to verify 
adequacy for specific loading conditions and local building codes.
