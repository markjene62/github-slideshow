STRUCTURAL CALCULATIONS - CANTILEVERED MONOPITCH AWNING
======================================================

DESIGN DATA:
- Material: 38x38x2mm SHS (Square Hollow Section)
- Steel grade: Assume S355 (fy = 355 N/mm²)
- Length: 7,500mm
- Projection: 800mm
- Slope: 35°

SECTION PROPERTIES (38x38x2 SHS):
- Cross-sectional area (A): 283 mm²
- Section modulus (Sx): 3,460 mm³
- Moment of inertia (Ix): 6,574 mm⁴
- Radius of gyration (rx): 4.82 mm
- Weight: 2.22 kg/m

LOADING ASSUMPTIONS:
===================

Dead Loads (DL):
- Self weight of steel frame: ~2.22 kg/m × 31.5m = 70 kg ≈ 0.7 kN
- Roofing (corrugated sheet): 0.15 kN/m²
- Total DL = 0.15 × (7.5 × 0.8) = 0.9 kN

Live Loads (LL):
- Maintenance load: 0.25 kN/m² (concentrated)
- Total LL = 0.25 × (7.5 × 0.8) = 1.5 kN

Wind Loads (WL):
- Uplift: 1.0 kN/m² (conservative)
- Downward: 0.5 kN/m²
- Total uplift WL = 1.0 × (7.5 × 0.8) = 6.0 kN
- Total downward WL = 0.5 × (7.5 × 0.8) = 3.0 kN

LOAD COMBINATIONS:
- Ultimate: 1.35DL + 1.5LL = 1.35(0.9) + 1.5(1.5) = 3.47 kN
- Wind uplift: 1.0DL + 1.5WL = 1.0(0.9) + 1.5(6.0) = 9.9 kN
- Wind down: 1.35DL + 1.5WL = 1.35(0.9) + 1.5(3.0) = 5.72 kN

CRITICAL LOAD CASE: Wind uplift = 9.9 kN

ANALYSIS OF SPINE BEAM:
======================

The spine beam acts as a cantilever with the kicker braces providing intermediate support.

Without kicker braces:
- Cantilever moment: M = WL²/2 = 9.9 × 0.8²/2 = 3.17 kN⋅m
- Required section modulus: Sx,req = M/fy = 3,170,000/355 = 8,930 mm³
- Available Sx = 3,460 mm³ << 8,930 mm³ (INADEQUATE)

With kicker braces (5 No. @ 1.5m centers):
- Kicker braces reduce the effective cantilever length
- Each kicker takes load proportionally
- Load per kicker ≈ 9.9/5 = 1.98 kN

Revised analysis with kicker support:
- Maximum span between supports ≈ 1.5m
- Moment in spine beam (between kickers): M = 1.98 × 1.5²/8 = 0.56 kN⋅m
- Required Sx = 560,000/355 = 1,577 mm³
- Available Sx = 3,460 mm³ > 1,577 mm³ (ADEQUATE)

KICKER BRACE ANALYSIS:
=====================

Each kicker brace carries: P = 1.98 kN (tension under uplift)

Tension capacity:
- Gross area = 283 mm²
- Net area (assume 85% gross) = 241 mm²
- Tension capacity = 0.9 × fy × Anet = 0.9 × 355 × 241 = 77.0 kN
- Utilization = 1.98/77.0 = 2.6% (ADEQUATE)

Compression capacity (if downward loads):
- Effective length = 1,131mm
- Slenderness ratio = Le/r = 1,131/4.82 = 235
- For λ = 235, reduction factor χ ≈ 0.15 (very slender)
- Compression capacity ≈ 0.15 × 355 × 283 = 15.1 kN
- Maximum compression = 5.72/5 = 1.14 kN
- Utilization = 1.14/15.1 = 7.5% (ADEQUATE but slender)

DEFLECTION CHECK:
================

Serviceability limit (DL + LL = 2.4 kN):
- Load per kicker = 2.4/5 = 0.48 kN
- Deflection of spine beam (simply supported over 1.5m):
  δ = 5wL⁴/(384EI) where w = 0.48/1.5 = 0.32 kN/m
- δ = 5 × 0.32 × 1500⁴/(384 × 210,000 × 6,574) = 0.61 mm
- Allowable = L/250 = 1500/250 = 6 mm
- δ = 0.61 mm < 6 mm (ADEQUATE)

CONNECTION DESIGN:
=================

Wall Connection (Spine Beam):
- Reaction = 9.9 kN (uplift)
- Use 4 × M12 bolts, 150mm centers
- Bolt capacity in tension ≈ 25 kN each
- Total capacity = 4 × 25 = 100 kN > 9.9 kN (ADEQUATE)

Wall Connection (Wall Plate):
- Compression force from kickers = 5 × 1.98 cos(45°) = 7.0 kN
- Use continuous fixing with chemical anchors
- Capacity adequate for compression loads

Welded Connections:
- All tube-to-tube connections: 6mm fillet welds
- Weld capacity >> applied loads (ADEQUATE)

SUMMARY:
========

✓ Spine beam adequate with kicker brace support
✓ Kicker braces adequate in tension
⚠ Kicker braces slender in compression (consider lateral restraint)
✓ Deflections within limits
✓ Connection capacities adequate
✓ Overall structural adequacy confirmed

RECOMMENDATIONS:
===============

1. Proceed with 38x38x2 SHS as specified
2. Ensure adequate wall fixings (M12 bolts minimum)
3. Consider lateral restraint for kicker braces
4. Professional structural engineer review recommended
5. Comply with local building codes and wind loading
